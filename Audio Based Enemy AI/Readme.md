# Audio Based Enemy AI

- Audio based enemy AI I created for a client on fiverr. He requested an enemy AI system that was barebones in nature but had the basic functionality of enemy locomotion based on the last sound heard.
- Sound emitters are spawned by the player on movement and zones can produce heavier sounds that scare the enemy hence activating its flee state.
- Light sounds cause the enemy to check out the sound source and wander a bit, and return to start position.
- Spotting the player leads to a chase sequence.
